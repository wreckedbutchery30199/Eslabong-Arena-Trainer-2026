## 🎮 About the Game

Eslabong is an Early Access action, strategy, simulation, tactical, and management game developed and published by shirowita. It released on August 24, 2026, for Windows PC. The game combines real-time arena battles with mercenary club management, seasonal leagues, cups, tournaments, and roster progression.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Eslabong**. This trainer provides single-player assistance for Eslabong's arena combat and mercenary club systems. It supports control over combat survivability, ability resources, gold, roster development, and progression values while preserving the game's core systems of recruitment, equipment, leagues, seasons, and tactical team building.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with fighter builds, test abilities and spells, accelerate club progression, explore seasonal leagues and tournaments, or reduce repetitive management tasks in single-player Eslabong sessions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### ⚔️ Combat

- **God Mode** — Prevents the controlled fighter from losing health during real-time arena battles.
- **Unlimited Mana** — Allows abilities and spells to be used without exhausting the controlled fighter's mana.
- **Unlimited Ability Charges** — Prevents limited-use abilities and combat resources from being consumed.

- **Unlimited Gold** — Keeps the club's gold available for recruiting mercenaries, purchasing equipment, and managing the roster.

- **Add Experience** — Adds experience to selected fighters so they can level up and improve across seasons.
- **Maximum Fighter Statistics** — Improves selected fighter attributes to support customized builds and stronger lineups.

- **Unlimited Relics** — Prevents relic capacity and selected relic-use limits from restricting equipped loadouts.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Eslabong allows direct control of a fighter during real-time arena battles. Move carefully, use positioning to avoid attacks, and aim abilities where timing and placement matter. Enable combat assistance only after entering an arena encounter, then disable it before leaving the match or changing control modes.

### Tools & Inventory

The club uses equipment and relics to customize fighters and create specialized loadouts. Open the roster or relic management screen before activating inventory-related options. Apply changes to one fighter at a time, confirm the updated loadout, and save before advancing to the next week or season.

### World & Environment

Progression takes place across an evolving mercenary club, dynamic markets, leagues, cups, tournaments, and recurring seasons. Gold supports recruitment and upgrades, while experience develops individual fighters. Make a backup of the save before modifying roster progression, renown, or seasonal values.

### Combat & Weapons

Battles can be played with direct control of the captain or handled through autobattle tactics. Fighters use class-based abilities, spells, status effects, healing, summons, projectiles, and defensive techniques. Activate health or resource options after combat has loaded, and avoid changing values during transitions, post-battle screens, or simulated matches.
